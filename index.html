Educational tool, not financial advice. Interest is charged once per payment period here, so quarterly, half-yearly and yearly figures are slightly lower than a real Australian bank would charge. Fixed rate. Interest is charged each payment period on the balance minus any offset; no fees. Every figure is checked against a payment-by-payment simulation before release. Curves above the spine step evenly from the equalised payment down to $0 (the reddest line); switch to mirror payments to pair each one with a payoff curve the same distance on the other side. Hover or drag across the chart to read any moment. Dashed lines are loans that take longer than the chart (from 35 up to 100 years, whichever are past its right edge): they leave the right edge still owing money, and they crowd closer to the spine the longer they take.
