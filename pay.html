Take-home pay

A rough estimate, not tax advice. It works out a full year as an Australian resident on one steady wage. Your real pay slips use the ATO's withholding tables, so each pay can differ by a few dollars, and tax time settles the rest.

Rates: ATO resident tax rates for 2025–26 and 2026–27 (ato.gov.au, updated 13 Aug 2026), low income tax offset up to $700 (ato.gov.au), Medicare levy 2% with the 2025–26 low-income thresholds of $28,011 and $35,013 (ato.gov.au; the 2026–27 ones aren't published yet, so they're used for both years), and study loan repayment rates for each year (ato.gov.au). Super guarantee 12%.

Not included: deductions, other income, the Medicare levy surcharge, family thresholds, seniors' offsets, salary sacrifice and the super cap on very high pay. Without the tax-free threshold it shows roughly what an employer would hold back; you get the extra back at tax time if this is your only job.
